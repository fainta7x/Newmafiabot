# Club connection stories: evidence and product interpretation

The owner requested new kinds of interesting club-wide patterns, rather than longer pair rankings. The implementation uses completed roster/role/result/event snapshots; it does not have verified voting, trust or night-action observations.

| Research principle | Bounded application |
| --- | --- |
| Newman, *The structure of scientific collaboration networks* (2000), https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0007214: shared participation can form a network | Count distinct shared-table participants. This is an analogy, not a validated Mafia model or proof of friendship. |
| Brave, Butters, Roberts, *Uncovering the Sources of Team Synergy* (2019), https://doi.org/10.3233/JSA-190248: complementarity requires modeling team production and confounding | Label wins as whole-team outcomes; never claim pair chemistry or individual causal impact from win percentages. |
| NIST, confidence limits for proportions, https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc241.htm: small samples need uncertainty treatment | Show games and different events; avoid strength claims. No confidence interval is calculated because repeated players/events are dependent and uncontrolled. |

Six views use Mafia-specific contexts: complete black trios, don–mafia, sheriff–citizen, balanced opposition, same pair together on both colors, diverse shared-table circles. Role-specific results are oriented; black trio identities survive role rotation. Opposing-team scores describe team outcomes, not personal duels. Source/event namespaces distinguish a club evening from a tournament with the same ID.

Display thresholds and ordering are product choices, defined in [BUSINESS_RULES.md](BUSINESS_RULES.md). Privacy removes hidden identities and their contribution to visible circle counts; it cannot reconstruct a complete black trio after removing a hidden extra seat. No production data or schema mutation is needed.

Deferred because the data does not support them: voting agreement, inferred trust, sheriff protection, betrayal, night coordination, causal synergy, opponent-adjusted strength and friendship networks. Do not manufacture these from final roles and wins.
