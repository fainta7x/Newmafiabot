# 2LA NOIRE — Art Direction: Player Key Art

Status: initial requirements approved, reference validation pending (2026-10-15).

## Goal
Create full-fledged illustrated premium video-game-quality key art featuring real club players, not photo collages, avatars or simple outfit overlays. Images may depict players in different worlds, genres, eras, costumes, environments, poses and stories.

## Approved direction
- Premium, authored digital illustration / AAA game key art; not photorealistic photography.
- A recognisable, coherent human likeness for each real player is mandatory across variations.
- Faces must be **drawn in the same artistic language as the body and environment**. No pasted live-action faces, photo textures or uncanny mismatched heads.
- Rich composition, art direction, lighting, environment and storytelling, not a plain portrait floating on a background.
- Allow stylistic variation between collections (noir, dark fantasy, cyberpunk, art deco, etc.), while preserving the player's identifying facial structure.
- Avoid generic AI gloss, plastic skin, over-beautification, unmotivated effects, template compositions, and low-effort stock-looking results.
- Preserve agreed reference framing/composition when explicitly requested; never silently crop or change aspect ratio.
- Generate multiple alternatives, review likeness and anatomy, then approve; do not mass-produce until a baseline identity and style are approved.
- Keep these illustrated key-art assets distinct from the modular avatar/outfit editor and existing seasonal rarity quotas.

## Proposed workflow (not yet implemented)
1. Collect a few consented player reference photos (ideally multiple angles) and user-selected art references.
2. Create a player identity sheet and one approved master illustration.
3. Produce 4–6 style studies with the same player; evaluate face likeness, anatomy, consistency, composition, premium finish.
4. Save prompts, references, versions, approvals and asset metadata for repeatable production.
5. Only then consider automated generation and gallery integration in the app.

## Acceptance checklist
- Player is recognisable without the name label.
- Face is an illustration, not a pasted photograph.
- Head, body, hands and lighting are anatomically and stylistically coherent.
- Image looks like deliberate premium game concept/key art, not generic AI output.
- Composition, aspect ratio and requested identity details are preserved.
- No publishing player likeness without appropriate permission.
