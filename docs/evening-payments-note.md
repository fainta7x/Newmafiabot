# Evening payment tracking

The organizer payment view belongs to the concrete evening workspace and remains editable after the evening is closed.

- Attendance/game close-out and payment collection are separate facts.
- Closing an evening may create debt for unpaid attended players, but it must not hide or freeze payment correction.
- Organizer/host/judge fee exemptions remain unchanged.
- Post-close payment confirmation must reconcile the financial transaction ledger so analytics and visible payment state stay consistent.
- Future online payment providers should update the same evening participant payment state rather than introduce a competing organizer workflow.
